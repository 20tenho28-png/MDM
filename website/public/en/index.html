Split, multi-split and VRF systems.

03 / ABOUT MDM

Since 1991.
HVAC technical support.

Based in Parque das Nações, MDM Assistência Técnica provides installation, maintenance and technical support for climate control, ventilation and associated electrical systems across Greater Lisbon.

01

Requirements assessment.

Identification of property type, occupancy, usage and climate control requirements.

02

Site assessment.

Assessment of mounting locations, pipe routes, drainage and electrical supply.

03

Technical proposal.

Equipment selection and definition of the scope of work in the quotation.

04

Technical support and maintenance.

Support for installed systems and preventive or corrective maintenance.
